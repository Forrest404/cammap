On keeping a map by hand

2nd September, 2026

There is a particular satisfaction in marking a place yourself rather than having it suggested to you. A pin dropped by hand carries the reason it was dropped, which is more than can be said for most of what turns up on a modern map.

This site does very little, and that is the point. Open the map, mark the spot, write a line about why it mattered. The list grows slowly. Nothing is recommended, ranked, or sold.
