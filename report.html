Report a camera

You need an account to send a report in. It takes one press and asks for nothing — see Account.

Seen a facial recognition camera that is not on the map? Send it in and someone will check it.

This does not put the camera on the map. Reports go to a queue to be looked at by hand; the map itself only ever changes by a commit to the site. Coordinates must fall inside Greater London.

The easiest way to get the coordinates is to open the map, find the spot, and read them off — or use any mapping app and copy the pair it gives you.
