    How to read this map

    Colour says what a camera is. The key above gives each kind its colour. A solid dot is a camera in use; a hollow ring in the same colour is a legacy site, one no longer in use; a dot filled in the non-functional colour is one reported as not working, whatever its kind. A wider, fainter ring round a dot means the record gave the site as an area – a borough, a district – and the pin sits at the middle of it, not on a known spot. Pull back and the dots pool into a glow, weighed by how many times the record has a spot being used – so a place a van has been sent twenty times reads hotter than one it visited once – and a camera on its own makes no glow at all. Every LFR van site sits behind the Legacy toggle: a van parks for a shift and drives away, so the record can say where one has been and never where one is now, and the map opens on the cameras fixed to something.
