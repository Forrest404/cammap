An account, without the usual parts

No email address, no real name, nothing about you. The site picks a username for you — two ordinary words — and you choose a password. That is the whole account.

With one, you can:

  • Save cameras. A star appears next to every camera in the list on the map page. What you save is visible to you and to nobody else.
  • Report a camera. Seen one that is not on the map, or one that no longer works? A new tab appears at the top for sending it in, and reports earn points on the leaderboard.
