Moderation

This page is for moderators.

Pending reports. Approving a new camera puts it on the map for everyone; approving a state report changes the camera it is about. Both give the reporter their XP. Reports that enough people agreed on have already gone through by themselves and are not here. Nearest first puts the reports that sit on top of a camera the map already has at the top - the likeliest duplicates, and the quickest decisions.

    Nothing waiting.

    Every camera the map knows about, seed and reported alike. Removing one hides it - it keeps its reports and can be put back. Adding one puts it on the map at once, with no report behind it and no XP for anyone. Move opens the map under a camera so its pin can be dragged onto where it actually is - the commonest thing wrong with a camera that is otherwise right. Edit corrects the rest of it: the name, the note, the kind and the state. Merge into… is for two rows that are one camera: the reports move to the row that survives and this one is taken off the map, with nothing deleted. Every change is recorded with who made it and what it said before.
