Report a camera

A report is a pin on the map, a few words, and a photo if you have one. It is checked before it goes on the map: once enough people report the same spot it goes on by itself, and until then a moderator looks first.

Reports are not available on this copy of the site.

Seen a facial recognition camera that is not on the map? Send it in. You can fill all of this in now; an account — two random words and a password, nothing else — is asked for when you send it, and nothing you have typed is lost.

Worth knowing: the coordinates you send are published with the camera if the report is approved. If you press the button while standing at the camera, that is where you were. Nothing records that it was you — but move the numbers to the camera rather than to yourself.

If the report is approved, these two become the camera's name and note on the map, for everyone, and they stay there: deleting your account later takes back the report, not the words it put on the map.

    Photos are re-saved in your browser before upload, which removes location and camera details from the file. Video is not accepted, because that cannot be done to a video here and a video carries the same details. Proof is seen by moderators only. 20 MB each at most.
