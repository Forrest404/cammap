Report a camera

You need an account to send a report in. It asks for nothing but a password — see Account.

Seen a facial recognition camera that is not on the map? Send it in. Once enough people report the same spot it goes on the map by itself; until then a moderator looks first.

Worth knowing: the coordinates you send are published with the camera if the report is approved. If you press the button while standing at the camera, that is where you were. Nothing records that it was you — but move the numbers to the camera rather than to yourself.

Photos are re-saved in your browser before upload, which removes location and camera details from the file. Videos are sent as they are, so check what yours contains. Proof is seen by moderators only. 20 MB at most.

Point at it on the map

Click the map to drop a pin, then drag it onto the camera. Zoom in as far as it goes — the closer the pin, the less a moderator has to guess.

Have coordinates from somewhere else? Type them into the two boxes opposite and the pin will move to them.
