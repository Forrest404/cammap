An account, without an account

Signing in here takes one press. There is no email address, no password, no username, and no way for anyone to work out who you are — including us. The account exists only in your browser and in a row of a database that holds nothing but a random identifier.

With one, you can:

  • Save cameras. A star appears next to every camera in the list on the map page. What you save is visible to you and to nobody else.
  • Report a camera. Seen one that is not on the map? A new tab appears at the top for sending it in.

A report goes to a queue for someone to check by hand. It does not appear on the map on its own — the map only ever changes by a commit to the site itself.

Signed in

You are signed in anonymously. Nothing about you was asked for or recorded.

Stars now appear beside every camera in the list on the map, and there is a new tab at the top for reporting a camera.

The account lives in this browser. Clear its storage, or use another browser, and you will get a new one — there is no password to sign back in with, which is the trade for not being asked for one.
