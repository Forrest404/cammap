About this place

This is a small and deliberately old-fashioned corner of the web. It keeps a map of London, a handful of notes, and very little else. There is no account to make, nothing to sign in to, and nothing at all is counted or sent away.

The map marks the places worth remembering: the turning that is easy to miss, the bench with the good view, the shop that is only open on a Tuesday. Each point has a name, and a note where there is anything more to say. Click one in the list to be taken to it.

It is a kept map rather than a shared one. The points are chosen by hand and written into the site itself, so the map you are looking at is the same map everybody else is looking at. There is nothing here for a visitor to add to, and nothing that remembers you were here.

For now it covers Greater London and stops at the edges, which is roughly as far as anyone should need to walk. The writing lives on the blog, which is updated whenever there is something to say and not before.
